This Privacy Policy explains how Discord-bot collects, uses, and protects your information when you use Discord application.

1. Information We Collect
To function properly, the App may collect the following data:

    Discord Data: User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs.

    Command Usage: Logs of the commands or interactions you use with the App.

    Content Data: We temporarily process message content when a command is used but do not store it permanently.

    Configuration Data: Settings you set up for the App within your server.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data to:

    Provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality.

    Troubleshoot bugs and monitor usage limits.

    Prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

    We do not sell your personal data.

    We may share data with third-party service providers (e.g., hosting providers like AWS or database services) solely to operate the App.

    We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Data Retention
We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the App's services. If you remove the App from your server, configuration data may be deleted automatically.

5. Your Rights
Depending on your location , you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. You can request data deletion by contacting us at raven.sellout@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your region). If we become aware of such data, we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify users of significant changes via our github page or the App's status page.

8. Contact Us
For privacy-related inquiries, please contact raven.sellout@gmail.com.
